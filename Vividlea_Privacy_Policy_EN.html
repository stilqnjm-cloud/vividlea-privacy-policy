Privacy Policy

How Vividlea handles information

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Privacy at a glance

  • Vividlea does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information through its own systems.
  • Vividlea does not use advertising or analytics to track users.
  • App progress and settings are stored locally on the device.
  • When Wallpaper Sync is enabled, background permissions and settings are used only to keep automatic stage and wallpaper updates working reliably.
  • Vividlea does not use these permissions to access unrelated personal information or interfere with unrelated device functions.
  • You remain in control of the permissions and system settings you choose to enable.
  • Vividlea does not operate a remote user-data backend.

1. Information we collect

Vividlea does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information through its own systems, including your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or analytics data.

2. Data stored on your device

Vividlea stores app-related information locally so its features can work. This may include your selected plant, growth stage, planting time, growth progress, wallpaper synchronization preference, language, Background Reliability setup state, and the minimum technical state needed to determine whether a scheduled background task completed as expected.

Vividlea does not send this information to the developer or to a Vividlea server. Android or Google system backup may handle app data according to your device and backup settings.

3. Wallpaper and background functionality

Vividlea uses Android system functionality to apply the wallpaper you choose. When Wallpaper Sync is enabled, Vividlea may schedule background work so the plant stage and wallpaper can update during long periods without user interaction. When Wallpaper Sync is disabled, Vividlea does not keep timed growth work scheduled; it recalculates the correct plant stage when the app is opened again.

For Background Reliability, while Wallpaper Sync is enabled Vividlea may locally check Android background permissions and capabilities that can be reliably verified, compare the expected and actual execution of its own scheduled background work, and use restriction information reported by Android when a background task is stopped. These checks are used only to maintain app functionality and are not used to track you.

Some manufacturer-specific Sleep or Deep Sleep settings cannot be reliably verified by the app. Vividlea does not claim to know which choice you made inside those system settings.

4. Payments and Google Play

Vividlea may be distributed as a paid app through Google Play. Purchases, payment details, Google account information, refunds, and transaction processing are handled by Google Play. Vividlea does not directly receive or store your payment card or banking information.

5. Advertising and analytics

Vividlea does not contain advertising and does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs to track users.

6. Data sharing

Because Vividlea does not collect personal data through its own systems, the developer does not sell or share personal data with third parties.

7. Data retention and deletion

Plant and growth data can be cleared with Reset growth. Background Reliability permission/setup state is not cleared by Reset growth. Other local app data normally remains until you clear Vividlea's app storage or uninstall the app. Android system backup may restore supported app data according to your device and backup settings.

Because the developer does not maintain a server-side user database for Vividlea, there is no Vividlea account or remote personal-data record to delete.

8. Security

Vividlea is designed to minimize data handling by keeping its app state local and by not operating a remote backend for user data. Background permissions are used only for the functionality described in this policy.

9. Children's privacy

Vividlea is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not require account creation or submission of personal information.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Vividlea gains new features or if legal or Google Play requirements change.
